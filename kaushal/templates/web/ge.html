Project Summary:

This interdisciplinary study at Koushal Viks University examined how awareness drives, local governance and student‑led clean‑up campaigns can reduce plastic waste and invasive weed growth in nearby water bodies. The project analysed data from partner communities, highlighted effective on‑ground practices, and identified areas where policy support is still needed for long‑term impact.
